## 5. Rejection Coverage Gap

The supplied encoders enforce record/tuple/field inventory and NFC, but do not
enforce the complete scalar rules. Examples:

- TEXT code-point maximum is not checked;
- DECIMAL grammar/range is not checked;
- BOOLEAN accepts truthiness instead of exact boolean;
- BYTES sourceKey maximum is not checked;
- UTF-8 auxiliary printable/control policy is not checked.

These omissions do not change C5-SNAP-01 bytes, but they mean Node/Python
equality is not sufficient proof of a spec-complete parser.
